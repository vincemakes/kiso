## A caution for whoever measures the next one

Two of my own scans said "zero fold lines" before the transcript said
otherwise, and both were wrong in ways worth writing down:

1. **Reading only the visible window.** A long answer pushes the fold
   into the scrollback, where a 34-row screen scan cannot see it. The
   fold was there the whole time.
2. **A needle that is not contiguous.** The driver waited on `✦ took `,
   which never matches: the row is written `\x1b[1m✦\x1b[0m took 83s`,
   so an SGR reset sits between the glyph and the word. The scenario
   ran to completion and then sat out its whole 300s wall. The
   compact-cli suite carries a comment warning about exactly this, and
   I walked into it anyway — a needle must be a run of bytes the
   renderer actually emits together.
