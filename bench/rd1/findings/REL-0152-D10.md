# REL-0152-D10 — a newline inside a paste submitted the panel answer

- **id:** REL-0152-D10
- **class:** input routing / content loss
- **severity:** P1 — pasted content is silently lost
- **agent:** kiso 0.15.4 and every version before it
- **found by:** owner question, 2026-08-25 ("你 t 的那个对话框可以粘贴内容吗")
- **status:** FIXED

## The answer to the question, before the fix: no

Pasting multi-line text into the type-your-own answer box SUBMITTED the
answer at the paste's first newline. The panel closed, the rest of the
paste fell into the composer behind it, and the typed prefix went with
it. Reproduced end to end: a 30-line trace pasted after typing
`here: ` produced the answer `here:` — five characters out of 445.

## The mechanism

The panel branches in `feed` handled CR/LF as Enter and `continue`d,
above the paste-aware newline normalizer that the main composer path has
always had:

```
if (this.#pasting || c === "\x0a") this.#insert(NEWLINE); else this.#submit();
```

Bracketed paste marks its own boundaries. Between them a `\n` is a line
of the pasted text and cannot be a keypress — that is what the brackets
are FOR. Three branches did not know it: the ask panel, the pick panel,
and the approval panel's amend/prose phase.

Note this predates the capsule work entirely. It is not a regression of
D8 or D9; it is a hole that was there for as long as those panels have
taken typed text, and it was found by someone asking whether the feature
worked rather than by any gate.

## Evidence

`packages/tui/tests/paste-capsule.test.ts`, four cases: a multi-line
paste does not submit; the answer is the typed prefix plus the WHOLE
paste; a short multi-line paste lands as itself with no early submit;
and a TYPED enter still commits — the guard is the paste, not the key.
