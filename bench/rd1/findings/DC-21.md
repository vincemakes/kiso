# DC-21 — the settle said the same thing twice, under two different clocks

- **id:** DC-21
- **class:** product shape / truthfulness
- **severity:** P2 — nothing breaks; the screen just says one thing
  twice and disagrees with itself about the number
- **agent:** kiso 0.16.6 (the shape R3d left)
- **found by:** the owner's dogfood ("two lines saying the same thing,
  the UI gets strange"), then an independent review (fable)
- **status:** FIXED (R3g working tree), owner-ruled

## What the settle looked like

```
 the question
✦ thought 17s · read 4 files · ran 2 searches · ctrl+r      ← the FOLD (compositor)
the answer text…
✦ thought 23s · read 4 files · ran 2 searches · in 12k out 900 · cache 88% · ctx left 41%   ← the RECAP (CLI)
```

Two lines, the same work terms, and two DIFFERENT numbers under one
word: the fold prints the kernel's measured thinking seconds (what
`endTurn` is handed), the recap prints the turn's wall clock. A turn
that thought for 1s and then ran a 40s shell said "thought 1s" on one
row and "thought 23s" on the next.

## The rule now

**The work is said once, by the line that can reopen it.**

- the FOLD says what the turn DID — in the place the work happened,
  with `ctrl+r` — and its seconds are the measured thinking time;
- the RECAP says what the turn COST — `✦ took 23s · in 12k out 900 ·
  cache 88% · ctx left 41%` — and `took` is the honest name for the
  wall clock it always carried.

A turn whose work did NOT fold (it spilled past the live region, or it
hit trouble, or it holds a single cell) keeps every one of its rows on
screen. The work is not lost by its absence from the recap; it is
standing right there. This is the case the compact-cli gate now pins by
name.

## The variant that was rejected, and why

The first plan was "exactly one line says the work": the recap would
carry the terms whenever the fold did not. It cannot be built the way
the code is ordered — `renderRecap` runs in the same synchronous
handler as `endTurn`, and the fold is decided a frame later, at commit
time. Predicting it eagerly does not work either: the settle frame is
itself where a big turn spills (every row of a turn is held until the
settle), so the prediction would say "folds" and then not fold, and the
work summary would appear in neither place.

The rule above needs no prediction: each line's content is decided by
the component that owns it.

## The public type

`RecapStats.tools`, `.edits` and `.byTool` are now OPTIONAL and are
still rendered when a caller supplies them — kiso's own CLI simply
stopped supplying them. An embedder of `@vincemakes/kiso-tui` keeps the
byte-for-byte historical row, so this is a patch, not an envelope
change.

## Two gates this produced

- `packages/tui/tests/render-recap-width.test.ts` — the recap is ONE
  physical row at any width. It was the one row nobody measured:
  written raw, it wrapped, and the second physical row was a fragment
  matching no cell format. R3g's longer terms made an 80-column wrap
  ordinary rather than rare, and the v2d interleaving lint caught it.
- The same gate pins that a degenerate width is not a width:
  `process.stdout.columns ?? 80` keeps the **0** a PTY without a winsize
  reports (0 is not nullish), and the recap cut itself to `✦ t…`.
