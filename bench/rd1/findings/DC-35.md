# DC-35 — ctrl+r printed the same expansion over and over

**Status**: FIXED
**Reported by**: the owner, from their own screen — three identical
blocks stacked up after a few presses
**Round**: R8b coda, 2026-09-01

## What was seen

A turn with ONE folded stretch. Pressing ctrl+r a few times appended
the identical four rows each time:

```
✦ expanded · listed 1 directory · 0 turns back
  list   (0.0s) · 81 lines · ctrl+r expands
  search (search_text failed: EPERM ...)
  └ search_text failed: ...
  end of expansion · ctrl+r opens the one before it
```

— and the footer said "ctrl+r opens the one before it" every time,
naming something that does not exist.

## The mechanism

The ring is not wrong. `expandNext` walks `#collapsed` newest-back and
clears `#opened` once every entry has been seen, which is R4/C1's rule
and is what makes the walk immune to the ring growing underneath it.
With a ring of ONE, the restart is immediate: every press finds the
same entry unopened again.

Reproduced at four presses, byte-identical every time.

## The fix

The bar is the BOTTOM of the transcript, not "ever shown". An
expansion earns its rows by showing something the screen does not
already end with, so `expandNext` remembers the last block it appended
and the cell count at the time; an identical block with no cell added
since answers `none` instead.

Re-opening stays available, which is the point of the key — once other
content has arrived the expansion has scrolled up, and the guard
clears itself the moment a cell is added.

## The false sentence it uncovered

The CLI answered both kinds of nothing with `[nothing to expand]`, and
in this case that was simply untrue: there WAS something to expand and
the reader was looking at it. The two cases now say different things,
and the honest one points at the key that does look back:

```
[that expansion is already the last thing on screen — ctrl+o opens the transcript]
```

## Gates

`packages/tui/tests/dc35-expand-repeat.test.ts` — four cases, two red
on the pre-fix tree: the second press does not repeat; five presses
append one block; re-opening after new content still works (a
regression gate); and a turn with nothing folded reports the OTHER
kind of nothing, so the two are not collapsed back together.

`compositor.test.ts`'s W15 carried the defect as an assertion ("the
SAME key appends again — the single collapsed entry cycles"). Rewritten
with the supersession recorded, and extended to assert what the case is
actually for: a committed cell can never toggle, only append, so once
other content arrives the same key appends again rather than rewriting
history.
