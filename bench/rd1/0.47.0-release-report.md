# 0.47.0 — release report (APPROVED)

The Graphite TUI. `tui/graphite` merged to `main` through #261 with
the required `check` green; this release adds only the version bump. The
owner ruled on 2026-10-07: the TUI ships as 0.47.0 and the wait release
moves to 0.48.0. This session runs the round, and the Task/Inbox session
reviews it independently.

- **Candidate:** 17 tarballs packed from the release branch's bump commit
  (every `dist` deleted and rebuilt first; the publishable list derived
  from the workspaces), installed isolated. The release smoke and the
  kill -9 demo ran on them.
- **Lockstep:** a new terminal surface and an additive runtime option →
  a MINOR under 0.x; 17 packages.
- **Kernel:** 2,192 / 2,200 lines, unchanged from 0.46.2.

## For hosts

### Action required

- **Only for a host that draws with `@vincemakes/kiso-tui` or
  `@vincemakes/kiso-tui-cells`.** None of the hosts we know of imports
  either. Both are rewritten for Graphite, and the change is breaking:
  - `kiso-tui` removes 7 exports: `idleStatus`, `runningStatus`,
    `StatusMeter`, `sessionRow`, `sessionCounterRow`, `atWindow` and
    `PANEL_KEYS_ROW`. The bar is now `statusBar`, and the live row is
    `workingRow` / `liveRow`.
  - 30 kept exports of `kiso-tui` and 16 of `kiso-tui-cells` change their
    types: the panels' views, the palette and the ground, the picker, the
    editor and the dock's body.
  - Such a host stays on 0.46.x, or moves to the new names.
  - Measured on the packed artifacts, against the installed 0.46.2, with
    the TypeScript checker over each package's `dist/index.d.ts`. A kept
    name counts as changed when its type's text differs, or the text of
    any of its properties' types. The reviewer's count over the type text
    alone is 15 and 9, a lower bound: an interface prints as its name, so
    a change inside it does not show.

### Observable behaviour changes

- **The CLI's pipe: none.** Every round kept the pipe's bytes, and the
  pipe-identity gates hold them.
- **Packages:** of the 17, four differ from 0.46.2 beyond their
  `package.json`: the two above, the CLI, and `kiso-runtime`. The other 13
  are byte-identical.
- **`kiso-runtime`, one change a host can see** (#196): a session named
  with the CLI's `/name` lists under that name. `SessionStore`'s title
  for it is the name, and the derived title is the fallback. The name is
  a third tenant of the session's `.meta.json` sidecar, beside `profile`
  and `summary`; their writes carry it through untouched. A session no
  one named reads exactly as before. The public surface stays 42 names.

### New optional capabilities (omit them and nothing changes)

- **`contextPolicy.tiers.onSummary`** (kiso-runtime, #254): an in-run
  summary told to the host as `/compact` tells its caller one — `start`
  (the tier, the covered rounds and their estimated tokens), `progress`
  (against the output budget), `end` (`kept` / `discarded` / `failed`,
  the context's estimate before and after). Display only: requests are
  byte-identical with and without it; a callback that throws changes
  nothing. The runtime's public surface stays 42 names.

## Findings (none blocks; all cosmetic, found in the agent's pass; fixed in 0.47.1)

- **0470-F1 — a cut note counts wrapped rows as lines.** A shell card's
  tail never opens in the middle of a wrapped line, unless no line start
  is within reach. Then it falls back to the plain tail, as its comment
  says. At 80 columns, a one-line output that wraps to six rows shows its
  last five, under `… 1 earlier line`; what is hidden is the start of the
  same line.
- **0470-F2 — `/compact` on a dock still prints `[summarized up to seq
  N]`.** The compaction round removed that raw row from the in-run path
  on a dock (§7.12, declared). The manual path writes it under its
  `COMPACTED` row as before. A pipe keeps it on both paths.
- **0470-F3 — `COMPACTED 1 rounds → 1 summary`.** The manual path's count
  is not made singular. The pipe's text carries the same.
- **0470-F4 — a refusal is said three times.** After esc on an approval:
  the head reads `denied by you · denied by user` (§7's form, whose
  reason is itself the words "denied by user"); the body carries the
  tool's result, `[Permission denied] denied by user`; and a row follows,
  `approval cancelled — treated as a denial`.
