## What is in it

| PR | what changed |
|---|---|
| #198 | The session store writes a streamed fragment (`text_delta`, `thinking`, `tool_call_input_delta`) without its own fsync; every other event is written and synced exactly as before, and that sync makes the fragments before it durable. ## For hosts

### Action required

- None.

### Observable behaviour changes

- **Streamed events reach a host sooner.** A run no longer waits for a
  disk sync after each `text_delta`, `thinking` or
  `tool_call_input_delta`; a long streamed tool argument or answer
  arrives without the stall. The events, their order and their content
  are unchanged.
- **The durability of a streamed fragment on its own.** A fragment
  becomes durable with the next synced event (the call's end, the text's
  end, the stop), not by itself. After a process kill nothing is lost,
  as before. After an OS crash or a power loss in the middle of a
  stream, the unsynced tail may leave damage the strict loader refuses
  (the session reports corruption rather than guess) where a per-event
  sync would have left at most a torn last line. Every correctness
  boundary — the turn's `stop`, `tool_execution_started` before any
  handler, the receipts, `tool_result`, `terminal` — is synced before
  the append returns, as before.

### New optional capabilities

- None.
